# Bishop reconstruction passes

Each archived pass contains its editable radial profile, neutral render, comparison report and original-white boundary overlay. The live builder and model are in `scripts/bishop` and `models/bishop`; history files are evidence, not an alternate build pipeline.

- **01-first-cut:** Established the turned construction and genuine Boolean slit. The initial mitre was too tall behind its finial and the base shoulder intruded into the neck.
- **02-proportions:** Shortened the mitre and lowered the base shoulder. Original-white silhouette IoU reached 0.9738. Internal landmark inspection showed that the socket lip and foot groove still needed adjustment.
- **03-internal-landmarks:** Widened and steepened the slit and adjusted socket, collars and groove. The shoulder rose too far in projection, so the next pass restored its measured contour.
- **04-before-head-correction:** Archived committed model, builder, profile, render and QA before the user identified the crown-like shelf and cylindrical band on the head. Original-white IoU was about 0.9626, demonstrating that outline agreement alone had missed the incorrect near-surface curvature.
- **05-continuous-head-pass-1:** Replaced the complete head meridian above the collar with one smooth Bezier curve. Removed the shelf and straight skirt. Original-white IoU improved to 0.9652.
- **06-before-button-correction:** Tightened the rounded head and moved its fullest point upward against the original. Near-side curvature is continuous into the collar transition. Original-white IoU was 0.9672. Archived before the user identified the sphere finial as incorrect.
- **07-before-continuous-button-join:** Replaced the sphere with a low convex button, rounded lip and short seat that intersects the head. The subsequent level-view review exposed the unwanted cylindrical seat. Contains the complete editable baseline and a matching level head render. Original-white IoU was 0.9679.
- **08-continuous-button-join-pass-1:** Removed the button seat and extended the convex head into its rounded underside, following the accepted side guide. Original-white IoU was 0.96726. Archived profile, reference render, level head render and comparison evidence.
- **Current, 09-continuous-button-join:** Slightly narrowed the button toward the accepted guide and buried the head cap farther inside the underside. No exposed neck, pedestal, ring or gap remains. Lower 65 profile points and real slit dimensions are unchanged. Original-white IoU is 0.96576. The actual evaluated side-section validator detects one broad head/button contact and no cylindrical run; the regression check also detects the rejected baseline's cylinder. Camera, export, topology and viewer checks pass. Metrics do not prove unseen geometry.
