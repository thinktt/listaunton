# Knight improvement mission

Recover the distinctive horse head in black candidate 2 as an editable 3D knight. At the fixed 54° camera, match candidate 2 and check the result against the original black Lichess sprite. From the side, aim for a beautiful, symmetrical carving with the same slightly abstract character and clean modern lines.

## What the references decide

Black candidate 2 is the main visual target for shape, internal features, wood and reflections. Compare the model with the original at native playing size as well as enlarged. Where the images clearly disagree on an identifiable feature, record the difference and follow the original for that feature. Where the elevated view leaves depth or curvature open, each sculptor interprets it from the visible carving. Exclude the imagined side render from worker references, fitting targets, judging and the improvement gallery. Compare side views rendered directly from the parent and candidate models.

## How to choose a winner

| Weight | Criterion | Look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 45% | Reference view | Compare the silhouette, muzzle taper, eye placement, ear sweep, mane and chest contours against candidate 2. Check that these features still read like the original sprite at board size. |
| 35% | Side profile | Judge beauty, symmetry and style together. Look for a gently conical muzzle with a resolved tip, a cheek that flows into the jaw, and a clean sweep from mane to neck. The open space beneath the jaw should give the head a graceful outline. Preserve the distinctive abstraction in the reference. |
| 15% | Form in the round | Inspect mirrored left and right profiles, a centered front view and a slow turn in clay. Both cheeks should carry comparable volume, the muzzle should sit on the centerline, and the ears should belong to the same carving. Look for pinching, skew or awkward transitions between views. |
| 5% | Finish | Check wood tone, grain direction and moving reflections against candidate 2. Award finish points for a back that looks carved from the same walnut as the front. Compare front and rear wood renders under the same studio lighting, then rotate through both flanks to check grain scale, flow, color and polish. Allow natural variation. Visible seams, abrupt changes in grain scale or a blank-looking back lower the score. The carving should read clearly in both walnut and neutral clay. |

Toby wants a sharp-edged mane and an eye that looks carved into this horse. Examine the edge of the mane from the side and rear quarter, then check the sweep against the elevated reference. Inspect the eye and its lids in a clay close-up as well as walnut. At board size, the eye should still sit naturally beneath the brow.

Assess symmetry in the geometry under even light. Directional highlights and natural grain can differ between the two sides. Use outline overlap to locate discrepancies and visual judgment to rank the carving. A candidate must improve the knight as a whole and preserve its recognizable reference view. Keep the parent when it is still the best design.

## Each round

Start from the latest accepted knight. Two GPT-6.2 Astra workers using Ultra reasoning each build one candidate from a separate copy of the same parent. Each worker explores a different design hypothesis. Render both candidates and the parent at the fixed 54° reference camera and the registered side camera with common scale, framing and comparison lighting. Compare their silhouettes at board size, then inspect enlarged wood and clay views.

The lead judges the parent and both candidates on the same criteria and records the visible reason for the decision. The winner becomes the parent of the next round. If neither candidate improves on the parent, the parent wins again.

After every completed round, update the Knight improvement tab in the repository and the existing local preview before starting either worker on the next round. Keep a chronological history of winners. Each entry identifies the round and the winner, gives the reason for the decision, and shows two walnut renders from the same validated, reopened winning scene side by side: the fixed 54° reference view and the registered side view. Preserve earlier entries and their images. When the parent wins again, add a new entry marked **Parent retained** and reuse its verified render pair.

Synchronize the accepted model and review assets, verify that both images load in the local preview, and commit the checked round. Begin the next round only once the new entry is ready for Toby to view.

Continue until Toby or lista-manager directs a stop. On that instruction, stop workers and renders, preserve the accepted knight, and record the state of the current round.
