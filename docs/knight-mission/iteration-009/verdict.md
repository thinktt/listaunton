# Checkpoint 009 · Walnut grain and chest reflections

Both black references show a dark chest plane between two narrow reflections. Checkpoint 008 had a broad, broken highlight on the near edge and little definition on the far one. The new scene uses four real area lights and two vertex-defined finishes to bring out those cuts. A measured correction to the walnut pigment increases the contrast of the intervening wood. In profile, the same carving and grain remain visible as the reflections turn away. [Fixed view](fixed.png) · [Actual model profiles](side.png) · [Chest study](chest/study.png)

The outline is measured. The four other grades and the holistic 86–90% estimate are visual judgments. The filled silhouette overlaps remain 98.54% for the original black and 97.19% for the mirrored white. At the registered fixed camera, mean absolute RGB difference against black candidate 2 fell from 14.64 to 12.85 over the common interior, from 20.03 to 14.63 across the neck, and from 6.01 to 4.97 over the turned base. In the isolated chest study the original black sprite error fell from 17.13 to 15.79 on its native grid. [Measurements](comparison.json) · [Scorecard and release check](scorecard.json)

The coarser trials made the turning look deeply pitted in profile. The accepted pigment uses 68% of the finer grain trial's change, retaining the established low-frequency walnut color. The detail on the visible front follows the selected reference. A continuous field around the turned geometry supplies the grain at the back. The chest and pedestal pigment edits affect separate atlas texels. The rotating viewer carries both varnish coverage and the local satin finish.
