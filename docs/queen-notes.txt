CURRENT REVISION — STACKED COLLAR PLATES

about .095 units apart, down from .171. The exposed axial spacer spans are

narrower, and the upper bead more subtle. The upper stem and lower crown
wall blend into the compressed stack. The crown wall keeps its full radius
at the upper plate instead of becoming pinched.

geometry. The matched camera is unchanged at 51.5 degrees. No comparison
images are shifted or warped. Full-silhouette overlap is approximately
93.8% (previously 94.0%); this is a shape interpretation with closer plates,
not a claim of exact recovery from a single image.

model. Previous now shows the immediately preceding, wider-spaced collars.
The parts map remains unchanged. Materials remain diagnostic; wood grain
has not yet been recreated.
