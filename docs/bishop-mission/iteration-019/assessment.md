# Bishop checkpoint 019 — recording audit and a small foot correction

The owner's blink recording shows white candidate 3 alternating with the
current neutral model. The internal forms still look inflated. The previous
87% interior rating was too confident; an almost coincident outside boundary
does not make the surfaces inside it correct. This checkpoint reassesses
interior form at approximately **80%**, with low confidence in that subjective
number. It is not a calibrated percentage of recovered geometry.

The recording does not justify uniformly shrinking the model. At unchanged
registration, the head and widest base extents are close, and the selected
enlargement's base is slightly wider than the model. `extent-audit.json`
records the actual row-region widths. The original white sprite remains the
authority when the enlargement differs. No reference, camera or display
transform is changed.

The first visual impression suggested that the whole head descended too far
into the collars. Tracing the projected ring positions showed that this claim
was too broad: the front junction positions are much closer than their shaded
appearance suggests. Curvature, rollover and apparent volume remain unresolved;
they must be assessed separately from the familiar outline and ring placement.
The inherited neutral rig still has localized fitted reflections. Additional
unsaved studio views remove those lamps, the wood pigment and relief to expose
the level profile and surface transitions.

Two collar-roll compression experiments and an inner-deck experiment were
examined. Compressing all three rolls makes the edges too sharp; the stronger
version approaches a knife edge. Lowering only the middle collar's inner deck
creates a clearer shelf in profile, but does not convincingly improve the
fixed reference view. None of these collar edits is accepted. Their configs,
neutral/wood renders, scope records and decisions are retained under `trials/`.
The rejected trial models stay in the ignored build directory.

The accepted change is deliberately smaller: carry the foot's existing maximum
radius farther up its wall and tighten the upper rollover. Only body controls
6-9 change from checkpoint 018. The foot reads as a straighter cylindrical
wall with a restrained upper bevel. Its underside, groove, flare, socket,
stem, collar stack, head, slit and button remain unchanged. This is **not** a
claim that the larger complaint has been fixed.

The final saved/reopened model is rendered at 512 fixed samples. The trial
improved original-white outline overlap from 98.694% to about 98.727%, and
selected-white overlap from 96.897% to about 96.929%. Original-black overlap
decreased slightly, from 97.789% to about 97.776%; white is the selected finish
and authority. Final values are in `comparison/metrics.json`. These small
changes describe the outside boundary only. `bounds-comparison.json` records
the small evaluated diameter change rather than claiming exact unchanged
bounds; height, camera and side-gallery normalization stay fixed.

The broader geometry mismatch is still unresolved. Particular concerns are
the collar face curvature and underside rolls, the apparent head fullness,
socket transition and flare/foot relationship. Before further edits, inspect
the corresponding physical surfaces and the original image's actual edges;
do not assume a reflection or grain band is a geometric crease. The button
front rim also needs a fresh placement/curvature audit. No high outline score
or successful topology check is evidence that these concerns are solved.

Working ratings are silhouette (measured, about 96.93%), interior form 80%,
wood color 83%, grain/surface 77%, and lighting 72%. Overall visual resemblance
is roughly 70-80%, rather than a near-perfect match. The interior reassessment
lowers the mean despite the local foot improvement. Color, texture and lighting
remain inherited and unfinished; their scores do not rise with a geometry edit.

The saved source archive, declared-control audit, closed-component/slit/button
checks, exact render receivers, UVs, pigment, lights, camera and exported mesh
must pass before delivery. Current fixed wood/neutral images, side/head/rear
views, model page and review are synchronized from the same checked scene.
The Node checks exercise the page code; no native browser visual inspection
is claimed. The owner's recording is independent evidence of the public view.

Mission files, other pieces, original images, registration and shared gallery
defaults are unchanged. Checkpoint 018 is preserved. The geometry goal remains
active and incomplete. No usage watcher, subagent, push or external publishing.
