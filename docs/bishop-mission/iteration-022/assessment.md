# Bishop checkpoint 022 — give the middle collar a distinct underside

The middle collar's rim and neck almost coincided in the saved 54-degree
projection. Evaluated-surface rays measure only **0.197 pixels** of front-center
overhang, compared with about **3.216 pixels** beneath the upper collar. That
helps explain why the middle plate looked like part of a rounded slope instead
of having the distinct underside visible in the reference.

The accepted edit narrows only neck radii **49 and 50**, by 0.4 and 1.2 original
profile units respectively. Every height and all other controls stay exact.
The rounded outer rim and upper deck retain their controls; the small joining
surface on the inner top of the lower collar follows subdivision naturally.
No global width, height, camera, reference or material adjustment is made.

The actual middle overhang becomes **2.435 pixels**. Its foremost edge moves
up only about **0.352 pixels**, while the upper and lower collar crest extrema
stay unchanged. These are independently sampled geometry measurements. They
are not a numerical fit to a photographic shadow, nor an exact recovered
cross-section. Fixed wood/neutral comparisons and a true-level neutral view
support the clearer separation while retaining a rounded, plausible turned rim.
The source supports a distinct edge; its exact hidden underside remains inferred.

Before this edit, three lower-head depth hypotheses were tested with coupled
radius/height changes that preserve the front control projection. The two short
transitions produce an unwanted band or ridge above the collar. Extending the
transition gives a convex control polygon, but the actual evaluated surface
still develops an unsupported band. None provides a convincing improvement
against the selected reference and side guide. All are rejected, including the
one that passes the usual scope/anatomy checks. An unchanged silhouette and a
valid mesh are insufficient to accept a new shape.

The actual slit boundary remains close to the original white and selected
white-3 opening; no cutter or head edit is accepted. Diagnostic rotations of
the evaluated geometry explore the less readable black original. They do not
establish a different source orientation. Dark grain and low contrast make
parts of its apparent slit ambiguous, so the investigation does not justify
changing the white-view cut or treating those bands as physical edges.

The final saved/reopened render retains approximately **98.74%** original-white,
**97.00%** selected-white and **97.78%** original-black outline overlap. Exact
values and the unchanged mask convention are in `comparison/metrics.json`.
The useful change is internal occlusion and surface separation, not an exterior
score increase. Overall bounds, 54-degree camera and source registration stay
fixed. The source archive is checkpoint 021.

Working scores are approximately **97.00 / 82 / 83 / 77 / 72**, mean **82.20%**,
with overall resemblance still about **70–80%**. Interior form increases modestly
for the now distinct middle rim; this remains a subjective, uncertain estimate.
Color, grain and lighting are retained and unfinished. Their scores do not
increase with this geometry edit. The apparent fullness of the head, collar
deck curvature and base transitions still require careful examination without
mistaking pigment or fitted reflections for shape. The rejected head trials
do not resolve that whole-image concern.

Saved/reopened scope and actual anatomy, full-depth slit, direct button contact,
historical neck regression, receiver positions/normals/UVs, packed pigment,
lights and export are checked. The accepted fixed wood/neutral, level/head/rear,
rotating mesh and review are regenerated from the same model. An additional
neutral level render uses broad, unfitted lights and changes no saved settings.
Node runtime checks exercise the viewer and review; native browser QA is not
claimed.

The geometry goal remains active and incomplete. Mission statements, source
images, candidate choices, gallery proportions, other pieces and historical
studies are untouched. No quota watcher, subagent, push or publication is used.
