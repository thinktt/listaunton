# Restoring the bishop’s original-view fit

The owner rejected bishop 027 because its head and button had moved in the original view. The working model, material, render, and interactive mesh are restored from bishop 024, commit `6d6944c`. Its registered outline overlaps the original white sprite by **98.774%** and white candidate 3 by **97.035%**. It is the best original-white outline fit in the saved series. The subsequent shared-base and shortening studies remain in Git.

The original camera, sprite alignment and fixed render are identical to 024. Both colors remain available in the review. This restoration also restores the earlier bishop-specific turned foot and seated finial. The restored physical height is **4.59201** model units.
