# Round 10 · Candidate B

The original black knight and black candidate 2 both show a rounded poll with one carved edge sweeping over the near ear. Viewed head-on, a horse’s mane like this needs a crest that rises in the middle. The previous knight gave two parallel reflections at playing size. B keeps the centered arch and cuts a shoulder into both flanks. Under plain lighting the edge remains visible on the clay. With the walnut finish, the extra stripe recedes. The point where the mane returns into the forehead still looks a little faceted.

Its lower muzzle follows the original to within 0.87 px mean absolute distance on the measured arc.

A has the cleaner transition between mane and forehead in profile. Its second bright band persists at the board angle. B’s upper shoulder is better defined, and the paired ears sit more naturally against the top of the neck. Six sections through the crest peak on the midline in both candidates. B’s largest mirror discrepancy at the sampled points is 0.0004 model units. The hairline and lower ear are distinct from both sides. The same packed walnut texture covers the front and back. The eye retains its round-nine shape and looks over-polished in profile. A carved upper lid and subtler reflection merit another pass.
