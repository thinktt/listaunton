# The cut at the end of the muzzle

A shallow line runs from the cheek toward the nose. Close to the tip it rises over a small notch and turns down to the edge. Both black references show the same sweep. Black candidate 2 resolves a narrow crescent of reflected light below the forward slope. The original shows a softer light in roughly the same place. The released knight has a short bright fleck on the cheekward slope. In its side view the forward end turns almost vertically into the nose. [Fixed and side correspondence](muzzle-side.png) · [Carving, pigment and polished surface](muzzle-crescent.png)

At 1254 pixels, the original’s brightest point below the notch is at (695, 510), black candidate 2’s is at (699, 514), and the round 15A parent’s is at (680, 502). The two references differ in the strength and extent of the light. In the original, 33% of the local bright contrast falls noseward of column 696. The fraction reaches 70% in the upscale and is below 1% in the model. The lower crescent in the model’s neutral-polish pass still catches on the cheekward side. Its pigment already carries a dark notch. [Registered measurements](muzzle-crescent.json)

The exterior profile deserves separate treatment. Across 112 shared columns, the underside of the current muzzle is within 0.87 pixels of the registered original on average. Black candidate 2 puts it an average of 5.46 pixels lower. A new interpretation can preserve the original’s conical envelope and work on the relief inside it. The exact depth of the notch and its appearance from the side are sculptural choices. [Underside comparison](muzzle-edge.png) · [Measured contour](muzzle-edge.json)

A future candidate could let a shallower cut approach the tip and give its lower lip a small rounded face that picks up light nearer the nose. Another could shape the cheek into a broader turn so the line stays readable as the knight rotates. Both can check the result against the original at board size and reopen the saved side under a moving light. These observations were made on the round 15A parent during the round 16 mane comparison.
